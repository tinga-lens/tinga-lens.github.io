## How to credit

Use the citation shown beside each map ("Cite this map"), or for the project as a whole:

> Adekilae, F. A. (2026). *Tinga Lens: Environmental monitoring for Ghana*. https://tingalens.org

Please also credit the original data provider named on each map. Tinga Lens results are
calculated from other people's datasets, and those providers must be credited as well.

## What is not covered

1. **Species records.** These come from GBIF. Tinga Lens uses only records published under CC0 or CC BY (records under non-commercial licences are left out). When you reuse them, credit GBIF and the occurrence download DOI shown on the Biodiversity page, and credit the dataset publishers as GBIF asks. The counts and statistics that Tinga Lens calculates from the records are covered by the paragraph above, but still credit GBIF.
   Tinga Lens does not publish IUCN Red List categories.
2. **Third-party data.** Where a file reproduces or is derived from another provider's dataset,
   that provider's licence and credit requirements still apply. They are listed in the README.
3. **Boundaries, basemaps and imagery** (geoBoundaries, OpenStreetMap, Esri) keep their own terms.
4. **Fonts and the map library** in `assets/` and `vendor/` keep their own licences (included beside them).
5. **The Tinga Lens name and logo** are not licensed here. See `NOTICE.md` and `TRADEMARKS.md`.
