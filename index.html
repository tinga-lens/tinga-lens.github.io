    How to read this map

    Each district shows its rainfall over the latest three months, compared with the same three months in every year from 1991 to 2020. A district is "very dry" when this year is among the driest 10% of those years, "dry" when among the driest 30%, and the same in reverse for "wet" and "very wet".

    Limits

    • This is a rainfall indicator. It does not measure crop damage, river levels or groundwater.
    • The rainfall estimate blends satellite and rain-gauge data on a grid of about 5 km. It suits district averages, not individual farms.
    • When the normal three-month rainfall is under 30 mm, the district is shown as "dry season" and not rated, because small differences would look like large percentages.
    • District boundaries are the 260 districts of 2019. Newer districts are not yet shown.
    • The map has not been checked against ground reports. Treat it as a guide, not an official drought declaration.
