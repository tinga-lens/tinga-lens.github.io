Three kinds of information

Every map on Tinga Lens says which of these it is.

Observed

What a satellite recorded, such as a fire detection or tree cover loss.

Environmental condition

An observation compared with what is normal for that place and time of year.

Modeled risk

A statistical estimate made by Tinga Lens. It is tested, but it is not an observation.
