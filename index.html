    What the labels mean

    Observed
    Something a satellite recorded directly, such as a fire or lost tree cover.
    Environmental condition
    A measurement set against what is usual for that district at that time of year.
    Published index
    A combined index published by another research group, averaged here by district.
    Modeled risk
    Our own estimate from a statistical model. We test it and publish the results, but it is not a measurement.
