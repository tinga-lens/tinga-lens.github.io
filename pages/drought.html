Drought

Rainfall and soil moisture in each district, compared with what is usual for the season.

About this module

Two products are shown. Rainfall compares the last three months with the same months in 1991–2020. Soil moisture compares the moisture in the top metre of soil with the same month in other years since 2015, and can be viewed every five days.

Rainfall and soil moisture do not always agree. Soil moisture responds within days, while the three-month rainfall total changes slowly. Read the methods.
