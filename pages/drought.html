Drought

Rainfall, soil moisture and the start of the rainy season in each district, compared with what is usual.

About this module

Two products are shown. Rainfall compares the last three months with the same months in 1991–2020. Soil moisture compares the moisture in the top metre of soil with the same month in other years since 2015, and can be viewed every five days.

Rainy season Experimental shows when the main rains started this year in each district and whether that is earlier or later than usual. It has been checked against 14 rain gauges and typically differs from them by about three weeks, so treat the dates as satellite estimates and the colours as a rough guide. It is not planting advice.

Rainfall and soil moisture do not always agree. Soil moisture responds within days, while the three-month rainfall total changes slowly. Read the methods.
