About this module

Where the records come from. Published record Every point is taken from the U.S. Geological Survey compilation Mineral Industries and Related Infrastructure of Africa (doi:10.5066/P97EQWXP, public domain). It combines three USGS sources: facilities from the Minerals Yearbook, deposits from USGS Professional Paper 1802, and exploration projects from the USGS World Minerals Exploration Database. TingaLens has not added, moved or estimated any location.

Three kinds of record, never added together. A mine, plant or field is an operating or once-operating facility. A documented deposit is a known occurrence of a mineral, whether or not it is mined. An exploration or development project is a company's prospect and may never become a mine. The same site can appear in more than one list, so TingaLens shows each list separately and does not give a single total of mines.

Status and dates. Facility status is the one USGS lists, and a facility is assumed active unless the source says otherwise. It reflects the source year, not today. Exploration records show the last year a project was verified as active, where known.

Not included. Small-scale and artisanal mining, mining licences and concessions, reserves and production figures, and many Ghanaian mines and prospects. These need records from the Geological Survey Authority, the Minerals Commission and the Petroleum Commission, and TingaLens does not yet use them. The page makes no statement about mining's effect on land, water or forests.
