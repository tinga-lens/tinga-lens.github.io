How urban growth and population intersect with flood-prone landscapes.

What is planned

This module depends on the flood module, so it comes after it. It will start with Accra, Kumasi, Tamale, Sekondi-Takoradi and Cape Coast.

  • Built-up area and urban growth Observed: how each city has expanded since 2000.
  • Exposure Modeled risk: how much new development and how many people are in areas the flood model rates as highly susceptible.

Every figure will state the year of the data it comes from.

Planned data sources

Global Human Settlement Layer (built-up area), WorldPop or GHSL population estimates, and the Tinga Lens flood products. All are publicly available.
