About Tinga Lens

An independent environmental monitoring initiative focused on Ghana.

Tinga Lens uses publicly available satellite observations, climate records and geospatial analysis to make environmental information easier to access and interpret. It monitors drought, fire, vegetation and forest change at national and district scales. Flooding and urban exposure are in development.

The platform is designed to support environmental awareness, research and evidence-informed decision-making. "Tinga" means land in Gurune, a language of Ghana's Upper East Region.

What it is not

Tinga Lens does not replace official warnings or emergency information. For those, consult the relevant Ghanaian authorities, such as the Ghana Meteorological Agency and the National Disaster Management Organisation.

Independence

Tinga Lens is an independent project. It is not affiliated with any university, company or government agency.

The platform uses only publicly available datasets and processing code written for this project. It contains no university research code, unpublished models or restricted data.
