About this module

Observed flooding Observed shows land that Sentinel-1 radar satellites saw under water during a named flood event, from the Copernicus Global Flood Monitoring service. Water that is normally there is not counted. Flooding under trees and between buildings is mostly invisible to radar, so the figures are a lower bound. Read the methods.

Flood susceptibility In development will estimate which land is prone to flooding. Nothing is published for it yet.

For flood warnings, follow the Ghana Meteorological Agency, the Ghana Hydrological Authority and NADMO.
