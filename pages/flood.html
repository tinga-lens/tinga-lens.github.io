Flood

Satellite-observed flooding and landscapes susceptible to inundation across Ghana.

In development

This module has no data yet. Tinga Lens does not show placeholder or example numbers, so this page stays empty of figures until real results are published.

What is planned

The module will keep two things separate:

  • Observed flooding Observed: areas that radar satellites (Sentinel-1) saw under water after heavy rain, with permanent water shown separately.
  • Flood susceptibility Modeled risk: an estimate of which land is prone to flooding, trained on past observed floods and using elevation, slope, distance to rivers, rainfall, soils and land cover.

Order of work

  1. Map observed flooding for a set of known flood events and check it against reports.
  2. Publish observed flooding by district.
  3. Train and test a susceptibility model on those observations, and publish it only after the test results are documented.

Planned data sources

Sentinel-1 radar (Copernicus), Copernicus elevation model, CHIRPS rainfall, public land-cover and soil datasets. All are publicly available.
