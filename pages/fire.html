Fire

Fires seen by satellite in each district, and an estimate of where fire is likely next.

About this module

Observed fire shows what satellites detected: the number of detections in each district compared with normal, and each detection from the last seven days as a point you can click.

Fire probability is a model estimate and is labelled as one. It is experimental. Most of its skill comes from the season, and it does not yet include temperature, humidity or wind. See how it was tested.
