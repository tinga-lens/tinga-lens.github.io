What helps most

  • A map looks wrong, or right. Tell us the district, the map and the date, and what you saw on the ground. A photo helps.
  • You work in farming, forestry, water, insurance or disaster response in Ghana. Tell us which figures you use and what is missing.
  • You want to use the data. You may, with credit. See how to cite and Data & methods.
  • You found a fault on the site. Say which page and what happened.

In an emergency

Tinga Lens is not a warning or emergency service and cannot respond to emergencies. For forecasts and warnings, contact the Ghana Meteorological Agency. For disasters, contact the National Disaster Management Organisation (NADMO).
