    Data coverage

    The number of species recorded depends on how much surveying has been done. A district with few records has been visited less; it does not have less wildlife. No record of a species does not mean the species is absent.

    About this module

    Recorded species Observed are counted from GBIF, the international database where museums, researchers and citizen-science projects publish their records. Tinga Lens places each record in a district and counts the different species. Read the methods.

    Threatened species are those the IUCN Red List rates Critically Endangered, Endangered or Vulnerable worldwide. Tinga Lens does not assign these categories. To protect them, this site shows district counts only and does not map individual record locations.

    Habitat suitability In development would be a model estimate of where a species could live. Nothing is published for it yet.
